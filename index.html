Apple Smartphones

Apple smartphones are mobile phones made by the company Apple which are identified by iPhones.
The first ever iPhone was released in 2007 by Steve Jobs. They are really popular around the world. They gained this popularity because of their design, confidentiality, and performance which also evolved the smartphone industry since then. Nowadays iPhones have advanced cameras, high resolution screens, and other useful features.
